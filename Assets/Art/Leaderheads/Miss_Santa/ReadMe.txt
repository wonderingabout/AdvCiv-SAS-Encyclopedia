This leaderhead is an imported model from Poser. Therefore, while many modifications of the models and artwork were made by me, I take no credit for the 3D model.

This leaderhead uses slightly modified Charlemagne animations. It's completely modular. All files in the zip-file are already put into the necessary folders, unzipping it directly in the Mods folder of BtS should result into a working mod. The leaderhead has a polycount of about 13400 triangles. 

Because there is no Santa civilisation she is an additional leader for the Holy Roman Empire. Her traits are organized & charismatic.
