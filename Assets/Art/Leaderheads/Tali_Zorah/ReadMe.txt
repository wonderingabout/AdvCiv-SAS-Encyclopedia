Tali'Zorah, Ladies and Gentleman, highly skilled Quarian Machinist. :)

This leaderhead is a converted model from Mass Effect 2. Therefore, I take no credit for the artwork and all rights are within BioWare of course.

This leaderhead uses the Isabella animations and needs all other Isabella files to work properly. It has a polycount of about 6000 triangles. The leaderhead is fully shadered.
