This leaderhead is a converted model from Fallout 3 (Face), Atlantica Online (Hair) and Diamond Club Online (Body). Therefore, although some modifications were done, I take no credit for the artwork and all rights are within the corresponding company.

This leaderhead uses the Boudica animations set. The leaderhead has a polycount of about 8000 triangles. No noshader version is included.
